## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a grounded, dignified, and calm personal finance experience engineered for young urban professionals, gig economy creators, and prudent accumulators across Nairobi. It deliberately rejects predatory fintech noise: there are no rapid ticker flashes, no speculative rocket or moon tropes, no anxiety-inducing red/green candlestick swings, and no garish neon utility greens.

Instead, the aesthetic merges warm minimalist editorial calm with tactile, modern accessibility:
- **Atmosphere:** Deeply human, steady, patient, and transparent. The interface feels more like a restorative journal or private pocket wealth vault than an exchange terminal.
- **Visual Stance:** Restrained minimalism paired with gentle surface containers. Generous negative space balances information-dense ledger records, giving users psychological breathing room around their money.
- **Ergonomics & Reality:** Tailored with intentional tap target clearances (minimum 48dp/52dp) and robust contrast ratios engineered for high-glare equatorial sunlight on ubiquitous mid-range Android screens.

## Colors

The palette establishes psychological safety through organic earth-mineral warmth and deep botanical teals, completely sidestepping both commercial M-Pesa neon greens and volatile crypto orange/gold cliches.

- **Primary Accent (`#0D7A73`):** Deep oceanic cedar/teal. Represents deliberate custody, grounding, and quiet prosperity. Used for affirmative actions, primary buttons, and key progress indicators.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm raw-linen canvas. Softens the harshness of pure white OLED screens, reducing eye fatigue during evening budget tracking. Paired with elevated container fills in `#F5F3EF` and `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Text & Structure (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Deep slate charcoal. Provides maximum legibility against the off-white ground without the severe clinical starkness of pure black `#000000`.
- **Secondary Slate (`#475569`):** Subdued slate for supporting metadata, transaction labels, and micro-copy.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Soft stone outlines that define hierarchy without slicing the screen into rigid cells.
- **Calm Surface Tints (`#E6F4F1`):** Subdued mint-teal wash used for callouts, goal progress cards, and peaceful state affirmations.
- **Intentional Restraint:** Feedback states avoid gambling-style saturated alarms. Positive growth uses quiet sage teal (`#115E59`), while warnings use muted clay ochre (`#B45309`) rather than fluorescent red.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing balanced geometric clarity with humane, open apertures. It maintains absolute legibility when rendering decimal-precise currency calculations (KES and satoshis) alongside straightforward financial advisory copy.

- **Numerics & Monospace Harmony:** Currency amounts and balance indicators rely on tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to prevent layout jump during updates and maintain vertical scan columns.
- **Scale Adaptability:** On mobile viewports below 400px, headline and display sizes contract smoothly to protect against awkward wraps in localized multi-lingual strings (English and Swahili terms like *Akiba*, *Hazina*, *Pochi*).
- **Weights:** Restricted to Regular (400), SemiBold (600), and Bold (700) to keep asset weight lean for fast network loads over cellular connections in the East African mobile ecosystem.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a mobile-first, fluid column structure anchored in a predictable 4px/8px base rhythm:

- **Mobile Rhythm (360px – 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with an outer canvas edge margin of `1.25rem` (20px) and gutters of `1rem` (16px). This ensures thumb navigation reaches critical actionable components while content remains safely distanced from curved device bezel edges.
- **Tablet / Large Handhelds (600px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Primary dashboards transition into side-by-side transaction ledgers and passive accumulation trackers.
- **Desktop / Web Shell (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at an intentional max content width of `1120px` to maintain the intimate feeling of a personal ledger rather than an enterprise spreadsheet.
- **Component Padding Flow:** Card bodies consistently utilize `1.25rem` (20px) internal breathing room, while compact line items utilize `0.75rem` vertical steps to preserve vertical rhythm during thumb-scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on stacked organic tonal tiers and ultra-diffused, ambient drop shadows rather than heavy synthetic layers or harsh multi-pixel borders.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** The foundational off-white page background (`#FAF8F5`). Completely non-reflective.
- **Card Surfaces (Level 1):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft pearl (`#F5F3EF`) with low-contrast borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Underpinned by a soft, warm ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Interactive Sheets (Level 2):** Elevated bottom sheets for MPESA deposit authorizations, transaction PIN prompts, and routine confirmation dialogs. Rendered with `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` and bordered softly.
- **Active State / Floating Action:** Gentle focus rings utilizing teal glow tints (`0 0 0 4px rgba(13, 122, 115, 0.15)`), eliminating harsh black outlines in favor of calm, tangible responsiveness.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, friendly, and organic geometry that reinforces emotional calm and approachable modernism:

- **Base Radius:** `0.5rem` (8px) for micro-elements such as chips, badges, and segmented toggles.
- **Medium Elements:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` (12px to 16px) on text input fields, balance selectors, and mini action buttons.
- **Major Cards & Surface Units:** `1rem` to `1.25rem` (16px to 20px, equivalent to `rounded-2xl`). This curvature eliminates sharp structural corners, making financial summaries feel comforting and approachable.
- **Action Buttons & Persistent Bottom Sheets:** Buttons use `1rem` (16px) or fully continuous pill curvature (`9999px`) where touch target friendliness is paramount. Bottom sheets utilize `1.5rem` (24px) top-corner radii.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0D7A73` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-lg`). Minimum height of 52px to ensure ergonomic tapping on mobile devices. Border radius of 16px (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Secondary / Supporting:** Tinted mint background (`#E6F4F1`) with `#0D7A73` text, no heavy border. Used for recurring savings setups or secondary flows.
- **Ghost / Neutral:** Transparent background with `#475569` text and an optional `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Disabled:** `#F1F5F9` background with `#94A3B8` text; strictly non-clickable without sudden color flashes.

### Cards & Savings Vault Containers
- Built on a crisp `#FFFFFF` foundation over the `#FAF8F5` canvas, bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and curved to a 16px–20px radius.
- Includes a dedicated **Accumulation Card** displaying satoshi reserves alongside KES parity using tabular layout figures, muted helper labels, and a quiet, single-tone progress ring.

### Input Fields
- Generous 52px height container with a `#FFFFFF` fill and a subtle `1px solid #CBD5E1` border.
- Floating or persistent top labels in `label-sm` slate text. Focus shifts the border to `#0D7A73` accompanied by an ambient teal focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 122, 115, 0.12)`).
- Input fields display currency prefixes (e.g., `KES` or `sats`) in fixed tabular monospace styling to prevent ambiguous input.

### Transaction & Ledger Lists
- Borderless rows separated by `1px solid #F1F5F9` dividers or standalone floating surface cards.
- Left-aligned round icon chip (40px) set against `#E6F4F1` mint containing clean, descriptive icons (e.g., direct bank transfer, M-Pesa paybill, steady accumulation rule).
- Right-aligned currency totals: Main fiat value displayed in bold `body-md` Slate 900; sub-values (sats) displayed below in `body-sm` Slate 500.

### Chips & Segmented Controls
- Minimalist pills with `0.5rem` radius. Inactive states use `#F5F3EF` with `#475569` text.
- Active states gently highlight with `#0D7A73` text on `#E6F4F1` surface, avoiding loud full-bleed fills for minor categorical tags.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons, & Toggles
- Custom rounded checkboxes (6px radius) and smooth switch tracks with a 28px height.
- Checked state transitions cleanly to `#0D7A73` with a pure white checkmark icon. Unselected states retain a clean `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` outline.

### Steady DCA / Routine Rule Builder (Product-Specific)
- A streamlined scheduling component designed for automating weekly or daily small-scale savings.
- Combines a frictionless cadence switcher (Daily / Weekly / Salary Day) with quiet numeric steppers, completely omitting aggressive trading language or market speculation indicators.
